How to Get There

By Car

Approximately 45 minutes from Dublin City Centre via the M3 motorway. Take Exit 6 for Trim.

By Bus

Bus Éireann services run from Dublin (Busáras) to Trim. The resort is a short taxi ride from Trim town centre.

From Dublin Airport

The venue is approximately 50 km from Dublin Airport. Rental cars and taxi services are readily available.
