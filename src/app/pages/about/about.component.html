The Divine Master

Sri Ganapathi Sachchidananda Swamiji is a revered spiritual master and the Peetadhipati of Avadhoota Datta Peetham®, Mysore. His divine mission is to spread the timeless wisdom of Sanatana Dharma, uplift humanity, and guide seekers on the path of devotion, selfless service and liberation.

With immense joy and gratitude, we are blessed to inform you that His Holiness Sri Ganapathi Sachchidananda Swamiji will be visiting Ireland. On this auspicious occasion, special programs will be organized for the grace and blessings of all.

His Divine Mission

Sanatana Dharma

Spreading the timeless wisdom and universal teachings of Sanatana Dharma across the globe.

Healing Through Music

Known worldwide for His healing music concerts that bring peace and well-being to listeners.

Selfless Service

Guiding devotees on the path of devotion, seva (selfless service), and spiritual liberation.

Environmental Conservation

Championing environmental causes including tree planting and animal welfare through various ashram projects.
